## What is Verdant?

Verdant is a free, offline-capable Android app that scans product barcodes and tells you instantly whether a product is vegan. No account. No API key. No nonsense.

Every ingredient is cross-checked against a database of **11,000+ known non-vegan substances**, including hidden additives like `E120`, `E904`, isinglass, and L-cysteine. You get a clear, color-coded verdict in under 2 seconds.

> **✅ Green** means vegan. **❌ Red** means not. **⚠️ Yellow** means uncertain, with the exact flagged ingredient shown so you can make your own call.

## Installing

> **Requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher**

1. Head to the [**Releases page**](https://github.com/themelone2/verdant-releases/releases/latest) and download the latest `.apk`

4. Install, open, and start scanning

## How It Works

| Step | |
|:----:|---|
| **1. 📷 Scan** | Point your camera at any EAN or UPC barcode. Hard to scan? Just type the numbers manually instead. |
| **2. 🔍 Check** | Verdant fetches the full ingredient list from Open Food Facts and cross-checks every single one. |
| **3. ✅ Result** | Get your answer in under 2 seconds. Green, red, or yellow, always with a full ingredient breakdown. |

---

## Features

- **📷 Camera + manual input** - Live viewfinder scanner with instant detection, plus manual barcode entry as a fallback
- **🧪 Deep ingredient analysis** - 11,000+ non-vegan ingredients covered, including sneaky additives like E120, E904, isinglass, and L-cysteine
- **⚠️ Uncertain ingredient flagging** - Ambiguous ingredients like "natural flavors" or glycerin are highlighted in yellow so you can decide for yourself
- **📋 Full color-coded breakdown** - Every ingredient shown as green, red, or yellow. Nothing left unchecked, nothing hidden
- **🕓 Local scan history** - Your last 20 scans saved directly on your device. No cloud, no sync, no account needed
- **📡 Offline-capable** - Core functionality works without an internet connection
- **🔒 Private by design** - All your data stays on your device, full stop
- **🆓 Completely free** - No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Ever
